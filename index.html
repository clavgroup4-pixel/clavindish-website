03 · For Merchants

Every business, a payment point.

The Clavindish card terminal feature lets businesses take payments straight from the app — or turn an unused card terminal into a Clavindish point of sale.

Payment link

Generate and send a link in seconds; customers pay by bank or card.

QR code

Display a QR code at the till, on a table or on an invoice.

Clavindish access code

Load a Clavindish access code onto an unused card terminal and start taking payments through the hub.

Settle into one account

Takings land alongside your business banking, with lower pay-by-bank fees.

06 · Regulation

Built on regulated foundations.

Rather than holding its own licence at launch, Clavindish intends to deliver regulated services through FCA-authorised partner and principal firms — so each product sits inside the UK regulatory framework from day one.

Partner model

Banking, payments, credit and investment services provided by authorised firms.

Safeguarding

Customer funds held with regulated institutions, not on Clavindish's balance sheet.

Consumer Duty

Clear terms, affordability checks and fair outcomes designed in.

Risk-first

Prominent warnings on crypto, shares and pay-later credit.
