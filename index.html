02 · Inside the hub

Your financial Allen key.

Money shouldn’t sit still between apps. Clavindish services work in tandem, so funds unlock the next step the moment they’re ready: paying, spreading, investing, buying a home and growing a business, working together to move you up your financial ladder with fewer hand-offs and less waiting.

  1. EarnSalary or takings land in your account
  2. MovePay and send at home or abroad
  3. SpreadSplit big purchases into clear payments
  4. GrowPut spare cash into shares or crypto
  5. OwnProve funds and buy property
  6. BuildRun and grow your business
Digital account

Your money’s home base

One account that every other service draws from and pays back into. Open everyday, savings and business accounts with FCA-authorised partner banks, and link the banks you already use so everything sits in one view.

How it works
  1. OpenVerify your identity in the app in a few minutes.
  2. ChoosePick everyday, savings or business accounts from partner banks.
  3. LinkConnect your other banks through open banking to see every balance.
  4. UseGet paid, spend, save and move money from one place.
What you’ll need
Name & addressDate of birthPhoto IDSelfie check
Key features
  • Several accounts, one loginEveryday, savings and business side by side.
  • All your banks in one viewLinked accounts show live balances and spending.
  • Instant notificationsEvery payment in or out, the moment it happens.
  • Card controlsFreeze, unfreeze and set limits in a tap.
Why on Clavindish

Your balance is the engine of the hub. Salary or takings land here and are immediately ready to pay, spread, invest or put towards a home, with no transfers between separate apps and no waiting for money to clear before its next job.

Accounts are intended to be held with FCA-authorised partner banks; eligible deposits protected by the FSCS up to the applicable limit.

Payment services are intended to be provided through regulated payment partners. Exchange rates move; the rate and fee are fixed when you confirm.

Pay later

Spread the cost, with every date in view

Split a purchase into equal payments at checkout. Every date and amount is shown before you agree, and repayments come straight from your Clavindish balance.

How it works
  1. ChoosePick Clavindish pay later at checkout, online or in store.
  2. CheckA quick affordability check before anything is agreed.
  3. PlanChoose 3, 4 or 6 payments and see every date and amount.
  4. RepayPayments are taken from your balance automatically, with reminders first.
What you’ll need
Clavindish accountAffordability checkOver 18
Key features
  • Clear scheduleEvery instalment date and amount, shown up front.
  • RemindersA heads-up before each payment is due.
  • Pay off earlyClear the balance at any time, at no extra cost.
  • One placePlans sit next to your balance, not in another app.
Why on Clavindish

Repayments come straight out of the account your money already lands in, so plans stay on track without topping up a separate app, and you always see how a purchase fits alongside everything else.

Pay later is a form of credit. Missed payments may affect your credit score. Subject to status and affordability; intended to be provided by an FCA-authorised partner.

Capital at risk. The value of investments can go down as well as up. Investments intended to be held through an FCA-authorised partner broker.

  3. TrackFollow searches, mortgage offer, exchange and completion.
  4. PayPay your deposit to your solicitor’s verified client account.
What you’ll need
Linked bank accountsSolicitor detailsProperty address
Key features
  • Verified proof of fundsNo printing statements or chasing PDFs.
  • Milestone trackerEvery conveyancing stage in one timeline.
  • Verified payeeDeposits go only to a checked client account.
  • Shared updatesAgent, solicitor and buyer see the same status.
Why on Clavindish

Your savings, investments and deposit already sit side by side, so proof of funds, exchange and completion can move together instead of waiting on statements, transfers and phone calls between different firms.

Concept in development. Conveyancing is carried out by your chosen regulated solicitor or licensed conveyancer.

06 · Regulation

Built on regulated foundations.

Rather than holding its own licence at launch, Clavindish intends to deliver regulated services through FCA-authorised partner and principal firms — so each product sits inside the UK regulatory framework from day one.

Partner model

Banking, payments, credit and investment services provided by authorised firms.

Safeguarding

Customer funds held with regulated institutions, not on Clavindish's balance sheet.

Consumer Duty

Clear terms, affordability checks and fair outcomes designed in.

Risk-first

Prominent warnings on crypto, shares and pay-later credit.
