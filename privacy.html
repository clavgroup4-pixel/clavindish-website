Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains how Clavindish Group ("Clavindish", "we", "us") collects and uses your personal data when you visit clavindishgroup.com, contact us, sign up through one of our Facebook or Instagram forms, register for one of our events, or receive our newsletter. We are committed to handling your data fairly, lawfully and securely under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Who we are

Clavindish Group is the controller of the personal data described in this policy. You can contact us about anything in this policy:

  • Email: info@clavindishgroup.com

2. The data we collect

  • Contact details: your name, email address and phone number.
  • Your answers on our forms: for example whether you will attend an event in person or by livestream, which topics interest you, and any message you send us.
  • Event information: your registration, attendance and any access or dietary needs you choose to tell us about.
  • Communication records: emails, texts and calls between you and us, and whether you open our emails.
  • Technical data: when you visit our website, basic information such as your browser type, device and pages visited, collected through cookies or similar technologies where used (see section 8).

We do not ask for, and you should not send us, bank details, card numbers, passwords or government ID numbers through our forms.

3. How we collect it

  • Directly from you, when you fill in a form on our website, contact us, or attend an event.
  • Through Meta (Facebook and Instagram) lead forms. When you tap a sign-up button on one of our ads, Meta may pre-fill the form with details from your profile. You can edit these before you submit, and nothing is sent to us until you press submit.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it to make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

5. Who we share it with

We only share your data with service providers who help us run Clavindish, under contracts that require them to protect it and use it only on our instructions:

  • HighLevel, our customer relationship management (CRM) system, which stores contact records and sends our emails and texts.
  • Meta Platforms (Facebook and Instagram), which hosts our lead forms and adverts.
  • Our website, email and hosting providers, including GitHub (website hosting) and Fasthosts (domain and email).
  • Event and livestream providers, such as the venue and the platform used to stream the event, only as needed to run it.
  • Professional advisers and authorities, where we are required to by law or need to protect our legal rights.

Some of these providers, such as HighLevel, Meta and GitHub, may process data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where this happens we rely on recognised safeguards such as the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. How long we keep it

  • Event registrations and enquiries: up to 24 months after our last contact with you, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Newsletter and marketing: until you unsubscribe or withdraw consent. We then keep a minimal record so we don't contact you again.
  • Records we must keep by law: for as long as the law requires.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct data that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your data;
  • ask us to restrict how we use your data, or object to our use of it, including for direct marketing;
  • ask for your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, for example by clicking "unsubscribe" in any email or replying STOP to a text.

To use any of these rights, email info@clavindishgroup.com. We will respond within one month. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113, though we would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

8. Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to make it work and to understand how it is used. Where cookies are not strictly necessary, we will only use them with your consent. You can control or delete cookies in your browser settings.

9. Keeping your data safe

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including access controls on our CRM and secure (HTTPS) connections. No system is completely secure, but we work to prevent loss, misuse and unauthorised access.

10. Children

Our website, events and forms are intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.

Clavindish is in development. No products are currently available to the public, and nothing on this website or at our events is investment advice.
